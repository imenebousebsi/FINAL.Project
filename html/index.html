ABOUT CRB

MORE THAN A CLUB. A LEGACY.

Chabab Riadhi de Belouizdad, known as CRB, is one of Algeria's most historic and successful football clubs. Founded in 1962, the club has built a strong identity based on passion, ambition, and excellence.

From generation to generation, CRB continues to bring together supporters, athletes, and champions who proudly represent the colors of the club.
